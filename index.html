Appendix

Definitions & data rules

  • In scope: Opportunities with Ab_others empty (NULL or blank).
  • Excluded (Sep): 10,553 September opps with Ab_others populated.
  • New opps: Created Time in reporting month (Apr–Sep 2026 series).
  • Calls / Sarv: Call Start Time in month (not Ab-filtered). Jayant Tuli excluded from all Calls metrics.
  • Detail month and Compare A vs B are mutually exclusive (toolbar buttons). Detail = one month on all cohort/insight cards; Compare = KPI A vs B and cohort on Compare B.
  • Slide 11: wins and booked revenue (₹) MoM by sales team member — second table is revenue.
  • Compare A/B drives executive & efficiency benchmark (slide 16).
  • Stars (slide 12): any rep with ≥1 cohort win in the detail month is tagged Star (win).
  • Performance join: Call Owner Name matched to Opportunity Owner Name for SV / wins.
